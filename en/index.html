Shinkansen Comparison

Shinkansen four-color train

White · Red · Yellow · Blue — four trains that symbolize the soul of Japan’s high-speed railways. From the main operators to the pioneers in inspection, each carries different missions and legends.

White Liner
White Liner

N700 series

のぞみ · Tokaido · Sanyo Shinkansen

The fifth-generation main vehicle of the Tokaido Shinkansen, "ダブルウイング・エアロ" has a very recognizable front shape. Equipped with a vehicle body tilting device, it can travel between Tokaido and Sanyo with commuter train-level acceleration performance.

top speed 300km/h
compile 16 両 / 1,323 people
Business starts July 2007
Operating range Tokyo ~ Hakata
Red Liner
Red Liner

E6 series

こまち · Akita Shinkansen

The Akita Shinkansen second-generation vehicle is painted in Akane red inspired by Akita's traditional "nano" and "kanto lights". It is composed of 7 mini cars but has a top speed of 320km/h, which is the same level as the E5 series. It is a new model of direct driving.

top speed 320km/h
compile 7両
Business starts March 2013
Operating range Tokyo～Akita
Yellow Liner
Yellow Liner

923 shape

Doctor Yellow · Comprehensive inspection vehicle

The “doctor” of the Shinkansen is famous for its bright yellow body and blue stripes. It does not carry passengers, but is responsible for testing tracks, electrical equipment and signaling systems. It is regarded by railway fans as a mysterious existence that "will make you lucky when you see it".

nature Non-business/inspection vehicle
Test items Track·Electrical·Signal
Painting yellow + blue stripes
Nickname ドクターイエロー
Blue Liner
Blue Liner

E5 series

はやぶさ · Tohoku·Hokkaido Shinkansen

The holder of the highest operating speed in Japan at 320km/h, it is an elegant combination of Tokiwa Green and Feiyun White. The 15-meter-long nose significantly reduces tunnel micro-pressure waves, and it is the first to create "Gran Class" top-level seats, ushering in a new era of Shinkansen.

top speed 320km/h
compile 10 両 / 731 people
Business starts March 2011
Operating range Tokyo ~ Shin-Hakodate Hokuto
